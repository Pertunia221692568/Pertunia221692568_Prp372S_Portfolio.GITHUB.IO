> LaunchPad.dev is a web platform that connects students with mentors, built by our team during Work-Integrated Learning (WIL).

## About the project

LaunchPad.dev was our WIL project: a full-stack web application where students sign up, verify their email, and connect with mentors, supported by an admin panel for managing content and contact details. We built it as a team using Agile, working in sprints, tracking work on a Trello board, and recording progress in an Agile logbook. This portfolio collects the evidence of how we organised ourselves and reflects on what I learned doing it.
