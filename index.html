About the project

What we built and why it matters.

Every year, thousands of ICT students graduate with technical theory but little practical experience. Many leave with a weak CV, no deployed project, and no interview confidence. LaunchPad.dev closes that gap by bringing four things into one affordable platform: a CV and GitHub audit tool, a Mock Interview Arena, Mentor Matching, and Portfolio Support.

Our survey of students showed that 87% felt their programme did not prepare them for a junior developer role, and 91% said they would use a structured career acceleration platform. We managed the work as an Agile team using Trello, daily check-ins, and a weekly logbook.
